Loopy's garden — game credits

Game implementation, level design and Canvas scenery: Ella blog theme.
Character: the Loopy pet selected and supplied by the blog owner, downloaded
through the Pets connector. The character artwork is not part of the MIT code
license below; third-party character rights remain with their respective owners.

Physics reference: JavaScript Tiny-Platformer, Jake Gordon and contributors
https://github.com/jakesgordon/javascript-tiny-platformer
The AABB overlap and fixed-timestep patterns informed this implementation.
The level, rendering, checkpoints, controls and game states were written for
this blog. No Mario or Nintendo game artwork, music or level maps are used.
